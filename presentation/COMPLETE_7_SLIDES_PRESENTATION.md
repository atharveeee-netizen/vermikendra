### Drag-and-Drop Instructions for PowerPoint:
1. Open your PowerPoint template (or a new presentation set to **16:9 Widescreen**).
2. For each slide from 1 to 7:
   - Create a blank slide.
   - Drag and drop the corresponding `.jpg` image from the project folder onto the slide canvas.
   - Snap the image to `Top: 0, Left: 0, Width: 100%, Height: 100%`.
3. Export as **PDF** and your official Hackathon submission is ready to upload!
